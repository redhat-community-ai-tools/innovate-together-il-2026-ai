# The AI Ready Enterprise

- **Time:** 10:05 (40 min)
- **Speakers:** Lior Haim; Noy Krief (Red Hat)
- **Materials:** slides-in-pack

## Abstract

Enterprise AI is ready for production today. A hands-on look at the platform stack that moves AI applications and agentic workloads off laptops into secure enterprise environments. Built for engineers and architects who keep systems running — live demos and practical system design, not buzzwords. Blueprint for running AI in production.

## Outline (from deck / speaker notes)

Framed as a story: the speakers “become” IT for a fictional Israeli mobile operator (**LemonTel**) that already modernized onto OpenShift — then get tagged by leadership to make the company “AI Enterprise.”

Recurring filter for every idea (two questions):

1. Can we ship this fast?
2. Will it survive **3 a.m.** (ops nightmare scenarios the audience already knows)?

The lemon / five-wedge visual is the day’s **blueprint**: five stages from “AI mandate” to a platform that answers both questions. Expect live demos and architecture choices aimed at platform engineers, not slideware.

*(Deck is highly visual; substance for this pack is the blueprint framing above plus the abstract. Do not invent LemonTel as a real customer or quote fictional metrics as facts.)*

## Takeaways to borrow

- Treat “AI ready” as a platform stack problem (identity, networking, GPU, observability, governance)
- Gate every AI idea with a **3 a.m. survival** test, not only a demo-happy path
- Prefer demo-backed architecture over slideware when evaluating vendors/stacks
- Map laptop prototypes to production controls early
