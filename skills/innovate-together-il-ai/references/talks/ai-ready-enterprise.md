## Abstract

Enterprise AI is ready for production today. A hands-on look at the platform stack that moves AI applications and agentic workloads off laptops into secure enterprise environments. Built for engineers and architects who keep systems running — live demos and practical system design, not buzzwords. Blueprint for running AI in production.

## Takeaways to borrow

- Treat “AI ready” as a platform stack problem (identity, networking, GPU, observability, governance)
- Prefer demo-backed architecture over slideware when evaluating vendors/stacks
- Map laptop prototypes to production controls early
